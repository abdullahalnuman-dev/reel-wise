01 Introduction and Scope

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") explains how ReelWise ("ReelWise", "App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you install or use the Android application listed on Google Play as ReelWise: Reels Counter.

ReelWise is a utility that helps users count short-form video scrolling activity on supported platforms, including Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, and YouTube Shorts. The App may also provide optional account-based synchronization, aggregate statistics/leaderboard features, notifications, and advertising.

This Policy is intended to describe the App's actual data practices and to be read together with the disclosures made in Google Play's Data safety section. If our implementation changes, we will update this Policy and the relevant Google Play disclosures.

02 Information We Process

2.1 Accessibility-service information

To automatically count reels/shorts, ReelWise can use Android's AccessibilityService after you explicitly enable the Accessibility permission in Android Settings. The service observes accessibility events generated by supported apps and uses relevant event/package/view information to determine when the user has moved through a short-form video feed.

ReelWise is designed to process this information for counting and related on-device functionality. We do not intentionally collect or upload the underlying content of the third-party apps you use.

👁️
Important accessibility disclosure

Android AccessibilityService can technically expose information displayed by another app. ReelWise requests this capability only so it can recognize supported reel/short scrolling events. ReelWise does not intentionally record screenshots, screen video, audio, passwords, private messages, comments, captions, or the content of videos for its counting feature. Do not enable Accessibility access if you do not want ReelWise to have this system-level capability.

2.2 Account and authentication information

If account features are enabled, ReelWise may use Firebase Authentication. Depending on the sign-in option you choose, Firebase may process:

  • An anonymous Firebase user identifier for anonymous accounts
  • Email address and authentication credentials for email/password accounts
  • Information provided by Google when you choose Google Sign-In, subject to Google's own policies
  • Account identifiers used to securely associate your ReelWise data with your account

2.3 Reel-count and statistics data

ReelWise stores information needed to provide counting, history, limits, progress and optional synchronization features. This can include:

Data Purpose Typical storage
Daily reel/short count Counting, limits, history and statistics Device; aggregate cloud sync where enabled
Platform identifier Separate Instagram/Facebook/YouTube statistics Device; aggregate cloud sync where enabled
Date/time period Daily statistics and history Device; aggregate cloud sync where enabled
Country/region code Optional aggregate leaderboard/ranking features Cloud only when that feature is enabled

ReelWise is designed so that cloud statistics contain aggregate usage information rather than the content of the videos you view. The App does not intentionally upload screenshots, captions, comments, direct messages, audio, or video files as part of reel counting.

2.4 Device and advertising information

When Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) is present, Google and its advertising partners may process device, advertising, diagnostic and interaction information as described in Google's applicable policies and the configuration of the AdMob SDK. This may include identifiers such as the advertising ID, IP address, approximate location inferred from network information, app/device information, ad interaction information and other signals used for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention or personalization where permitted.

03 Accessibility Service and Supported Platforms

The AccessibilityService is an optional Android system capability that you enable yourself. It is required only for the automatic counting feature. Without it, ReelWise may not be able to detect scrolling events in supported third-party apps.

ReelWise is not affiliated with, sponsored by, endorsed by, or officially connected to Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, Google, Meta, or any other third-party platform whose content may be counted by the App. Their names and marks belong to their respective owners.

  • ReelWise does not need your social-media passwords to count reels/shorts.
  • ReelWise does not log in to Instagram, Facebook, or YouTube on your behalf.
  • ReelWise does not intentionally copy or upload the media content you watch.
  • You can disable the AccessibilityService at any time from Android Settings.

04 Firebase Services

ReelWise may use Google Firebase services to support authentication and optional cloud synchronization/leaderboard features. The specific Firebase services used by the released version should match the declarations made in Google Play Console and this Policy.

4.1 Firebase Authentication

Firebase Authentication is used to create and secure ReelWise accounts when you use anonymous authentication, email/password authentication, or Google Sign-In. Firebase processes the identifiers required to authenticate the account. We do not receive or store your Google account password.

4.2 Cloud statistics / leaderboard

If cloud synchronization or leaderboard functionality is enabled, ReelWise may send limited aggregate statistics associated with your account, such as date, platform and reel count. A country/region code may also be used for country-level ranking where that feature is enabled.

☁️
Data minimization

ReelWise is designed to keep the cloud dataset limited to what is necessary for account functionality and aggregate statistics. The counting system is not intended to upload screenshots, video files, captions, comments, private messages, or the text content of third-party social-media apps.

05 Advertising — Google AdMob

ReelWise may display banner advertisements using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). When advertising is enabled, Google may collect and process information necessary to provide, measure, secure and improve advertising services. Depending on the user's region, consent, device settings and Google's advertising configuration, this can include advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, device/app information, ad interaction data, diagnostics and fraud-prevention signals.

AdMob is operated by Google. Google's processing is governed by Google's applicable terms and privacy documentation. You can review Google's privacy information at Google Privacy & Terms.

5.1 Consent and personalized advertising

Where required by applicable law and Google's consent requirements, ReelWise may use Google's consent mechanisms to obtain and record your choices regarding advertising and related data processing. Depending on your region and choices, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

We do not sell your personal information ourselves. However, third-party advertising services may process data under their own policies when ads are served.

📢
Google Play disclosure

If the released build contains AdMob or any other advertising SDK, the Google Play Console "Contains ads" declaration and Data safety answers must accurately reflect that advertising SDK's data practices.

06 Local Storage and On-Device Data

ReelWise stores app settings and counting information locally on your Android device so that the app can work efficiently and preserve your progress. Depending on the released version, this may include daily limits, counting history, platform-specific counts, preferences, notification settings and synchronization state.

6.1 Local database and preferences

  • Local counting/history data remains on your device unless a cloud feature explicitly synchronizes an aggregate dataset.
  • App preferences and settings may be stored locally.
  • Local data can normally be removed by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling ReelWise.
  • Data that has already been synchronized to your account is governed by the cloud retention/deletion rules in this Policy.

6.2 Background work

ReelWise may use Android background scheduling mechanisms such as WorkManager to perform permitted synchronization or maintenance work. Background execution does not by itself mean that ReelWise continuously uploads accessibility content.

09 Children's Privacy

ReelWise is not specifically designed as a child-directed app. Its core purpose is to help users monitor their short-form video scrolling activity. We do not knowingly request or intentionally collect personal information from children for purposes unrelated to providing the App.

Because ReelWise may use AccessibilityService, Firebase authentication and advertising, parents/guardians should consider whether the App is appropriate for their child. If the target audience selected in Google Play includes children, the released implementation must also satisfy Google's Families Policy, including its requirements for SDKs, advertising, identifiers and child data.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us in a way that violates this Policy, please contact us using the information in Section 14 so that we can review the matter.

10 Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the nature of the information handled by ReelWise. These measures may include Android application sandboxing, authenticated Firebase access, database security rules, encrypted network transport such as HTTPS/TLS, restricted service access, and secure release-build practices.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to reduce the amount of data handled and protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

🔐
What ReelWise does not intentionally upload for counting

Screenshots, screen recordings, video files, audio, captions, comments, direct messages, passwords and the general textual content of third-party social-media apps are not intended to be uploaded to ReelWise's servers as part of the counting feature.

11 Data Retention and Deletion

11.1 Local data

Local counting history and preferences are retained on your device until you delete them, clear the App's storage, or uninstall ReelWise, subject to Android's own backup/device behavior.

11.2 Account and cloud data

If you create or link an account, account information and synchronized aggregate statistics may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the service, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce agreements. When you request account deletion, we will delete or de-identify associated data that we are required and able to delete, subject to lawful retention requirements and reasonable technical deletion periods.

11.3 Advertising data

Data processed by Google Mobile Ads is retained and handled according to Google's applicable policies and retention practices rather than being controlled entirely by ReelWise.

12 Your Rights and Choices

12.1 Accessibility control

You can turn ReelWise's AccessibilityService access on or off from Android Settings at any time. Turning it off prevents automatic accessibility-based counting.

12.2 Account deletion

If account functionality is available in your version of ReelWise, you may use the App's account deletion feature where provided. You may also contact the privacy address in Section 14 to request deletion of your account and associated personal data.

12.3 Local data deletion

  • Use ReelWise's available history/settings deletion controls.
  • Use Android Settings → Apps → ReelWise → Storage → Clear data, where supported.
  • Uninstall ReelWise to remove its local application data from the device, subject to Android's system behavior.

12.4 Advertising choices

Advertising choices may depend on your region, consent status, Android settings and Google's available controls. Where applicable, ReelWise may present a consent message allowing you to make choices about personalized advertising and related data processing.

12.5 Privacy-law rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights such as access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or withdrawal of consent. To exercise a right, contact us using the privacy contact information in Section 14. We may need to verify your request before completing it.
