01 How Account Deletion Works

ReelWise allows registered users to permanently delete their ReelWise account from within the application. The deletion option is available from the Account section of Settings and is designed so that users do not need to contact the developer to make a deletion request.

✓
Direct in-app deletion

You can initiate account deletion yourself at any time while signed in to ReelWise. The deletion action is separate from uninstalling the application.

02 Steps to Delete Your Account

Follow these steps while you are signed in to your ReelWise account:

01

Open ReelWise

Launch the ReelWise app on your Android device and make sure you are signed in to the account you want to delete.

02

Open Settings

Go to the Settings section of the ReelWise app.

ReelWise → Settings
03

Open Account

Select the Account section to access account-management options.

Settings → Account
04

Select “Delete account”

Tap the Delete account button and complete the confirmation shown by the app.

Settings → Account → Delete account
05

Confirm the deletion

Confirm the destructive action when prompted. Once confirmed, ReelWise processes the account deletion and signs the deleted account out.

⚠️
Deletion is permanent

Deleting your ReelWise account is intended to be permanent. Make sure you are deleting the correct account before confirming the action.

03 Data Deleted

The following categories of data associated with your ReelWise account are intended to be deleted as part of account deletion:

Data categoryDeletion treatment
ReelWise account / authentication record Deleted as part of the account-deletion process.
Account profile information Account-associated profile data stored by ReelWise is deleted.
Reel usage / tracking data Account-associated usage information stored by ReelWise is deleted.
Daily limits and app preferences associated with the account Account-associated cloud data is deleted. Device-local preferences may also require clearing app data or uninstalling the app if they are stored locally.

04 Data Retention

ReelWise does not intentionally retain account-associated data after a completed account deletion request, except where retention is necessary to satisfy a legal obligation, resolve a security issue, prevent fraud or abuse, or establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Any data that must be retained for one of these limited purposes is restricted to what is necessary for that purpose and is retained only for the applicable period before secure deletion or anonymization.

ℹ️
No general retention period is intended

For ordinary account data, deletion is intended to remove the data rather than keep it for a separate user-request waiting period. Limited legally or operationally required records may remain for the period required for that specific purpose.

05 Local Device Data

Account deletion and app uninstallation are different actions. Deleting a ReelWise account removes the account-associated data handled by ReelWise. Data that exists only in the Android app's local storage may remain on the device until the app's local data is cleared or the application is uninstalled.

  • Open Android Settings → Apps → ReelWise → Storage to clear local app data, where supported by your device.
  • Alternatively, uninstall ReelWise to remove its local app storage through Android's normal app-data lifecycle.
  • Device-level settings controlled by Android or other applications are not deleted by ReelWise.

06 Third-Party Services

ReelWise may use third-party infrastructure and services to provide authentication, data storage, notifications, advertising, or other app functionality. Account deletion applies to data controlled by ReelWise; it does not mean that your accounts with unrelated third-party services are deleted.

  • Your Instagram, Facebook, and YouTube accounts are not controlled by ReelWise and are not deleted.
  • Third-party service providers may process limited technical information according to their own privacy policies and applicable retention rules.
  • Advertising-related information, if processed by an advertising provider, is governed by that provider's policies and controls rather than by ReelWise's account record.

07 If You Cannot Access the App

The primary account-deletion method is the in-app option described above. If you cannot sign in or the Delete account option is not working, use the support contact published on the ReelWise Google Play listing or the developer's official support channel and request account deletion. Include only the minimum information necessary to identify the account.

🔐
Never send your password

ReelWise support should not require your account password to process a deletion request. Do not include passwords, authentication codes, or other sensitive credentials in a support message.

08 Contact

For questions about account deletion, privacy, or a deletion request that could not be completed in the app, contact the ReelWise developer through the support contact published on the Google Play listing.
